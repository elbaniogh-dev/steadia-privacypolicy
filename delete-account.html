Delete Your Steadia Account

Steadia, developed by Ntech Studio, allows you to request deletion of your account and associated cloud data at any time.

How to Request Deletion

To request deletion of your account and all associated data, email us at elbaniogh@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request", using the email address associated with your Steadia account.

What Gets Deleted

Upon request, we will permanently delete:

  • Your account credentials (email/password)
  • All cloud-synced business data (transactions, stock items, contacts, reports)
  • Any receipt photos stored in the cloud

Retention Period

Deletion requests are processed within 30 days. Local data stored only on your device is not affected unless you also uninstall the app.
