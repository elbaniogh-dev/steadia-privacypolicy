Steadia Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 22, 2026

Steadia ("the App") is developed by Ntech Studio ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your choices.

1. Information We Collect

Steadia is designed to work primarily offline. The following data may be collected depending on how you use the App:

  • Account information: If you choose to create an account (optional), we collect your email address for authentication purposes via Firebase Authentication.
  • Business data: Sales, expenses, purchases, stock items, and contacts that you manually enter into the App.
  • Photos: If you attach receipt photos to transactions, these images are stored locally on your device and, if you have an account, may be synced to secure cloud storage (Firebase).
  • Contacts you add: Names and balances of customers/suppliers you manually add within the App. We do not access your device's phone contacts.

2. How We Use Information

Data you enter is used solely to provide the App's core functionality: recording transactions, tracking stock, managing contacts, generating reports, and — if you opt in — syncing your data across devices via your account.

3. Data Storage

By default, all data stays locally on your device. If you create an account and enable cloud sync, your data is stored securely using Firebase (Google Cloud infrastructure) and is accessible only to you via your account credentials.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal or business data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

5. Your Choices

Account creation and cloud sync are entirely optional. You may use Steadia fully offline without providing any personal information beyond what you choose to enter as business records.

6. Data Deletion

You may delete your data within the App at any time. If you wish to delete your account and all associated cloud data, contact us using the details below.

7. Children's Privacy

Steadia is intended for business use by adults and is not directed at children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
